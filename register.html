Register your Cooperative

Join the FORUS network.

Registration starts with a short cooperative application. Tell us about your cooperative and primary contact, and we will guide you through the next steps.

Start your application

Takes a few minutes. Have your cooperative and primary-contact details ready.

Who should register

Start with your cooperative’s primary contact.

The application should be completed by a representative or primary contact who can provide the cooperative’s details and continue the setup process if the application is approved.

If you are a member rather than the cooperative’s primary contact, explore FORUS and speak to your cooperative leadership about joining.

  • A representative who can provide the cooperative’s details
  • A primary contact who can continue setup if approved

What happens next

Four clear steps.

Before you begin. Have your cooperative and primary-contact information ready. The live form determines the exact fields required.
  1. 1

    Submit your application

    Provide the cooperative and primary-contact information requested in the registration flow.

  2. 2

    FORUS reviews the details

    The cooperative application is reviewed before secure setup continues.

  3. 3

    Continue secure setup

    If approved, the primary contact is guided through the next service and identity steps using the approved contact details.

  4. 4

    Start with what is relevant

    Available services depend on the cooperative, market and implementation scope. FORUS will guide you on what is appropriate.

Questions

Before you apply.

Does registering switch on every FORUS service?+
No. Availability can depend on your market, implementation and service conditions. FORUS will guide you on what is appropriate for your cooperative.
What happens if my application is approved?+
Your primary contact is guided through the next secure setup, service and identity steps.
I am a cooperative member. Can I register the cooperative?+
The application should be completed by an appropriate representative or primary contact.
